## 4. The 32 × 32 core: floorplanned and globally placed, its placement stopped by the job's time limit

`neo_core` at NCHK = 4 -- 1,024 data PEs, 128 check PEs, the feeder, the accumulator, the two requantizers, the
sequencer pair and the three buffer macros: 1,745,167 cells (223,501 µm²) and 65,114 µm² of macros, 80 % of the cells
in the array -- ran in run 182 with its ORFS targets ending at global routing (`designs/neo_core/config.mk`). It got
through the floorplan and global placement. The placement's design-rule repair did not finish inside the job, so there
is no CTS, no global route and no timing (`pnr_neo_core.md`); the brief's "placed and globally routed" is not met.

What took the time: `repair_design` fixes the SDC's design rules (max fanout 32, max transition 0.16 of the period) on
every net before any timing repair. On the 16 × 8 core it took 2.8 min for 373,783 cells; on the 32 × 32 core more than
275 min for 1.9 M -- a hundred times the time for five times the cells. Its own table shows the work bunched: the
first million nets it visited needed 2,136 repairs, the next 800,000 needed 41,063 (with 94,810 buffers). Nets that fan
out across the array give it buffer trees to build -- the asynchronous reset alone reaches 204,960 of the 208,992
flops. Until that repair has run, the placed netlist's timing is no figure of the design (OpenSTA at global
placement: −416 ns of unbuffered fan-out).

Two floorplan items for the next run, whatever its machine: the feeder's macro faces the accumulator's back across
10 µm with half of its 1,048 pins (the `wbuf_mem` / `tile_nic` situation of section 1: a `macro_place.tcl` turning it
before global routing), and the 4,116 pins on a 725 µm die.

Power ran on the floorplan database (`neo_report` takes the newest database a run leaves): 330 mW with the recorded
activity, 142 mW at the default, the cells' power alone -- no wires, no clock tree. The recording is the 16 × 8 core's,
folded onto the 32 × 32 by index: 164,440 of the 208,992 flops took a recorded bit (20,425 by name, 144,015 folded),
the rest the default (`power_drop043.md`).

## 5. What routing the 32 × 32 core would need

The measured reference is the 16 × 8 core on the same hosted runner (4 cores, 16 GB, 6 h per job; `c16_neo_core`, run
183): 373,783 cells placed on a 298 µm die; placement 18.0 min (2.8 of them in `repair_design`), CTS 4.6 min, global
routing 35.1 min and 5.95 GB, detailed routing 177.6 min and 15.2 GB -- 4.2 h of the job's 6 and nearly all of its
memory. The 32 × 32 core is 5.1 times its cells on 5.9 times its die. Scaled linearly, global routing would take ~3 h
and detailed routing ~15 h; the memory does not scale down to the runner (detailed routing peaked at 15.2 GB for the
16 × 8 core and for its array alone, global routing at 13.4 GB on the bank's 1.2 mm die), and placement did not scale

3. **Less for `repair_design` to do, either way.** The reset distributed as a registered tree (per row or per quadrant)
